About Prashant Subedi

Aspiring AI/ML Engineer & Researcher · Gaindakot, Nepal

I'm Prashant Subedi, an aspiring artificial intelligence and machine learning engineer and researcher from Gaindakot, Nepal. I'm currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology (CSIT) and spending most of my time learning how modern machine learning actually works — from the underlying mathematics to training and evaluating real models.

My focus: AI & Machine Learning

My core interest is machine learning and deep learning. I'm working through the foundations that serious ML depends on — linear algebra, probability and statistics, and optimization — and pairing that theory with hands-on practice in the Python data and ML ecosystem: NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, and deep-learning frameworks like PyTorch and TensorFlow. My goal is to become an AI/ML engineer who understands models deeply enough to build, debug, and improve them rather than just call an API.

  • Supervised & unsupervised learning
  • Neural networks & deep learning
  • Math foundations for ML
  • Data wrangling with Pandas/NumPy

AI research

Beyond coursework, I'm drawn to AI research. I read papers, try to reproduce experiments, and build small projects that turn ideas into working prototypes I can measure and improve. Reproducing results and understanding why a model behaves the way it does is, to me, the most rewarding part of the field — and the habit I'm most deliberately building.

Building for the web

Alongside AI/ML, I develop for the web. Frontend development isn't my headline specialty, but it's a genuinely useful complement: it lets me ship and demo the models I train by wrapping them in a clean, usable interface — end to end, from experiment to something people can actually try. I'm comfortable with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and tools like Git, Django, Docker, and Linux.
