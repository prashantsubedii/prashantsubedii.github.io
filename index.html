About Me

I'm Prashant Subedi, an aspiring AI/ML engineer and researcher currently pursuing my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology. My focus is on learning the mathematics and engineering behind modern machine learning — from the fundamentals of statistics and linear algebra to training, evaluating, and deploying real models that solve meaningful problems.

Day to day, I'm deep in machine learning and deep learning — working through supervised and unsupervised methods, neural networks, and the Python data stack (NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, and PyTorch/TensorFlow). I'm especially drawn to AI research: reading papers, reproducing experiments, and building small projects that turn ideas into working prototypes I can measure and improve.

I also build for the web, which lets me ship and demo the things I train — I'm comfortable on the frontend and can wrap a model in a clean, usable interface end to end. Whether it's a training experiment or a web app, I approach every problem with curiosity, rigor, and a steady growth mindset.
