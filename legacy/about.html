About Prashant Subedi

Aspiring AI/ML Engineer & Researcher · Gaindakot, Nepal

I'm Prashant Subedi, an aspiring AI/ML engineer and researcher from Gaindakot, Nepal, currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology (CSIT). My focus is machine learning and deep learning, and understanding how models work deeply enough to engineer them into real software people can actually use.

AI/ML engineering & research

My central interest is artificial intelligence and machine learning, not just as a user of tools, but as someone who wants to understand how models work. I study the foundations of ML and deep learning, work through the Python AI/ML stack (NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, and PyTorch/TensorFlow), and read and reproduce papers to see what genuinely holds up. What drives me is applied research: taking an idea from a paper and engineering it into working software.

  • Machine learning & deep learning
  • NLP & large language models
  • Reproducing & evaluating research
  • Data analysis with Python

Software engineering

On the engineering side, I design and build full-stack applications end to end. That means Python and Django on the backend, JavaScript on the frontend, backed by relational databases and REST APIs. I lean on solid computer-science fundamentals, clean code, and Git to build software that is reliable and maintainable, not just software that runs. Bringing a model out of a notebook and into a real, usable product is where my engineering and research sides meet.

How I work

Whether I'm training a model or shipping a feature, I follow the same loop: understand the problem, build a solution, measure it, and improve. I approach every project with curiosity, engineering rigor, a collaborative mindset, and a steady drive to keep learning.
